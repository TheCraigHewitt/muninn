# Answers are limited to everyone who will see them, not just the asker

An answer may only use Memories visible to every Principal in its Answer Audience. When the Slack bot answers in a channel, the audience is the whole channel, so an asker's access to restricted transcripts never shows up in a public reply. An answer drawing on the asker's full access is only available ephemerally (seen only by the asker) through a slash command. The obvious design answers with the asker's permissions wherever they ask, and that would leak restricted Memories to everyone else in the channel.
